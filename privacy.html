Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Clipwise HQ app ("the app"), which Clipwise HQ uses to publish its own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (account ID, display name).
  • The list of videos the app posted, and their public statistics (views, likes, comments).
  • OAuth access tokens, so the app can post on the account's behalf.

How it is stored

All data stays with Clipwise HQ. Access tokens are stored privately and securely. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties, other than the platform API the data came from.

Deletion

Revoke the app's access in the platform's connected-apps settings and the tokens stop working. Email clipwise13@gmail.com to ask for any stored data to be deleted.
